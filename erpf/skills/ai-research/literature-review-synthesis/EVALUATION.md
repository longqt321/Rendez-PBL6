# Evaluation Rubric: Literature Review and Evidence Synthesis

| Dimension | Level 1: Novice / Fail | Level 2: Competent / Pass | Level 3: Exemplary / Proficient |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Citation Traceability & Integrity** | Hallucinates non-existent papers, authors, or venues; fails to verify DOIs. | All cited papers are verified real publications in indexed venues. | Rigorous multi-hop snowballing; verifies peer-reviewed status, open-source code availability, and historical context. |
| **2. Structural Clustering** | "Laundry list" format (chronological author-by-author paragraphs without synthesis). | Groups papers by general categories (e.g., "Deep Learning methods" vs "Traditional methods"). | Groups papers thematically by underlying mathematical/algorithmic mechanisms with clear trade-off boundaries. |
| **3. Critical Limitation Extraction** | Accepts claims of prior art uncritically; ignores missing baselines or unstated assumptions. | Identifies common limitations stated in the authors' own limitations sections. | Uncovers unstated computational assumptions, dataset distribution limits, and evaluation harness flaws across entire clusters. |
| **4. Research Gap Validity** | Claims novelty based on trivial engineering variations (e.g., "Nobody tested Model X on Country Y data"). | Distinguishes between engineering delta and scientific gap. | Formulates a falsifiable conceptual research gap rooted in fundamental mechanism trade-offs. |
